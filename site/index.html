Filler

Android storage filler

Fill a storage device's free space with temporary files, then delete them.

Choose built-in storage or a memory card, see the free space Filler measured and the part it keeps free, and fill the rest. Filler deletes only the files it wrote - your own files stay as they are.

Filler is an app for Android and Wear OS that fills the free space of a storage device with temporary files and deletes them again. It is for the moment you want the free space of a memory card or a phone used up and handed back, with your own files left alone.

Memory card→ fill, keep 1 GB free →only Filler's files deleted

Install Filler

One Google Play listing carries the Android app and the Wear OS app.

Install from Google Play, opens in a new window Source code, opens in a new window
  1. Install Filler from Google Play on your phone, tablet or Wear OS watch.
  2. Open Filler. On a phone or tablet, tap Choose folder to pick a storage or a folder, or keep Filler's own folder; check the free space and the part kept free.
  3. Tap Start filling and confirm. When the run ends, or when you stop it, Filler deletes its files.
Before a run

Filler keeps 1 GB of the free space untouched by default. If you lower that amount, the system and other apps have less room while the run lasts, and the confirmation says so.

What Filler does

Choose where, keep room

Pick built-in storage, a memory card or a folder on it in the Android folder picker, or start at once in Filler's own folder. Filler keeps part of the free space for the system: 1 GB by default, as little as 10 MB if you lower it before a run. One confirmation names the folder, the amount and the part kept free before anything is written.

Follow a run, stop at any moment

While Filler writes, you see how much is written, the percentage and the time left. Stop whenever you like: the files written so far are deleted.

Clean up after a pulled card

Filler deletes only the files it wrote and keeps a list of them. After a stop, a write error, a restart or a removed memory card it offers to delete what is left as soon as the storage is back.

Fill on a schedule

On the phone, save a schedule and Filler fills a folder at the set time with the app closed, even after a restart, then deletes the files. A scheduled fill keeps the usual 1 GB free, runs at most once a day, and every run is listed with its time and result.

Fill from a watch

On a Wear OS watch, Filler fills its own folder on the watch and keeps going after the screen goes dark. A notification shows the progress and a Stop button.

Details

01Any device, and the watch

Filler works with touch, keyboard, mouse and D-pad, in portrait and landscape, on phones, tablets and other Android devices, and on Wear OS watches. One application identifier, com.sza.filler, covers the Android app and the Wear OS app. The Android app's interface is in 13 languages.

02Privacy and permissions

Filler has no internet access: no account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking. Nothing leaves your device, and the developer receives none of your data. Access to a folder is something you grant yourself, one folder at a time, in the Android folder picker.

The phone app asks for five permissions, all so that a scheduled fill runs on time with the app closed. The watch app asks for four, all so that a fill keeps going while the screen is dark. The full text is the privacy policy.

03What Filler does not do
  • It deletes only the files it wrote itself; it never opens, reads, copies or changes your own files.
  • It does not check whether a memory card really has the capacity it claims.
  • It does not recover deleted files and does not guarantee secure erasure of data.
  • It works with the storage of the device and does not fill network or cloud storage.

05Support

Report a problem or ask a question in the issue tracker. A useful report says which app it is (the phone or tablet app, or the Wear OS app), the Android version and the device model, the Filler version shown in the Android app settings, what you did, what you expected and what happened instead.

The issue tracker is public, so leave out anything private. Filler sends no logs or diagnostics anywhere, so a report holds only what you write in it.

For anything private, and for a security problem, write to sza@ukr.net.

Filler is maintained by one developer. Replies come as time allows, with no fixed response time.
